# Privacy

Ash Studio Recorder works entirely on your computer.

- **No account, no upload, no analytics.** The app makes no internet connections.
  Links you click (for example in documentation) open in your browser.
- **What is recorded.** Only what you choose: one display or window, and the
  camera and microphone when you turn them on. Cursor position and clicks are
  recorded while you record, so the editor can draw the cursor and zoom.
- **Keystrokes.** Off by default. When you turn on "Log keystrokes", key codes are
  saved for the keyboard overlay. The keyboard is not monitored at all while the
  option is off.
- **Where data goes.** Recordings are folders on your disk (see
  [install.md](install.md#where-things-are-stored)). Logs contain warnings and
  errors only, never screen content, and stay local.
- **Removing data.** See [uninstall.md](uninstall.md).
